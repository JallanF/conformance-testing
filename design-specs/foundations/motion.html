Panels arrive in sequence — a short offset per panel, not a long one. The end-state is the base style and the animation runs from hidden, so under reduced motion (and in print, and in a PDF export) the content is simply there. Every cue has a static equivalent.

The rules

Every cue is one-shot. Never blinking, pulsing, or looping an attention animation. Nothing animates continuously inside a table or at rest.

Transitions preserve context: expansions unfold, views slide rather than cut. A confirmed-good transition earns one beat of ceremony — then the interface returns to calm.

There is no real-time push in v1: data arrives by fetch. When a refetch visibly changes a value on screen, it may take a brief one-shot highlight that settles back to its resting state.

Reduced motion is honoured with a static equivalent for every cue — including the ceremony.
