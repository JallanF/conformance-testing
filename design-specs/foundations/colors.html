Colour

Dark is canonical and the default; light is a first-class peer. Colour is punctuation — spent on action and status, never on wallpaper.

The one rule

Amber is punctuation, never wallpaper. It appears in exactly four places: the one primary action per view, the focus ring, text selection, and the quiet accent hover wash. It is never a large surface tint, never a border colour, and never decoration. Secondary emphasis uses the inverse pill — never a second amber.

Surfaces — depth is tint, not shadow

--background The canvas. Everything sits on it.
--card Panels. Lifted above the canvas by tone alone.
--popover Transient layers — menus, dialogs.
--border The hairline. Separation without boxes.
--border-strong Hover, and meaning-bearing boundaries.
--muted Recessed fills.

The amber, and its one companion

--primary The one action per view. The focus ring. Selection.
--accent The quiet hover wash. The only amber that touches a surface.
--inverse Secondary emphasis. Never a second amber.

The status ladder — five roles

Delivered
success — confirmed-good transitions.

Quote accepted, booking confirmed, customs released, delivered, payment settled. An in-transit shipment running on schedule is not green — resting-normal stays neutral.

ETA revised
info — noteworthy, but normal.

An ETA revision needing no action, a document ready to download, a rate-change notice.

Documents due
warning — needs attention soon.

A quote nearing expiry, documents missing before pickup, payment coming due, customs requesting paperwork. Shown filled — the colour-independent weight channel.

Customs hold
destructive — blocking or critical, and destructive actions themselves.

Customs hold, payment failed, booking rejected; cancel, delete. Shown filled. A critical alert never lives in a disappearing toast.

Tracking stale
stale — broken data trust.

A tracking feed lost, or aged past its freshness threshold. Stale composes: stale-and-was-warning shows stale. Stale data is never presented as current.

Never colour alone. Every status above carries its glyph and its label — that pairing is how status is read, not a fallback for people who cannot see the colour. Soft status washes are derived at point of use from the role colour and the surface the wash sits on — --card on a panel, --background only on the canvas; status tints are never minted as new values.
