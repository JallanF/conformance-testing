Shape & space

Space is this product's confidence. Content is staged like a product page, never crammed like a terminal — and depth comes from tone, not shadow.

One silhouette per control class

Actions are pills — the signature control silhouette; chips and badges share it. Inputs, panels and dialogs are near-square on the radius scale. Never mix silhouettes within a class. Note the emphasis budget above: one amber action. The secondary is the inverse pill — never a second amber.

Radius scale

--radius-sm 6px — chips, small inputs
--radius-md 8px — inputs
--radius-lg 10px — panels, the base
--radius-xl 14px — dialogs, large cards
--onyx-radius-pill Actions, chips, badges

Sections stack in a deliberate vertical rhythm on this unit. Density is never user-switchable and never differs per view. Forms and reading views hold a constrained measure; data views use the full content width. Interactive targets never fall below 24px.

Elevation — tint, then hairline. Shadow only when floating.

--background — the canvas

--card — a resting panel. Lifted by tone and a hairline. No shadow.

--popover — a transient floating layer. This is what a shadow is for.

Never give a resting panel a shadow. Depth is tint layering — background beneath card beneath popover — plus hairline separation. A hover may strengthen a hairline to border-strong; it never adds a shadow. shadow-sm/md/lg belong to transient layers only — menus, popovers, dialogs — over the overlay scrim. And glow is not elevation: it is a rare celebration accent on a confirmed transition, never ambient.
