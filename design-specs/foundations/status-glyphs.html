Status glyphs

Each of the five status roles has exactly one canonical glyph, used everywhere. The glyph is what lets status survive without colour.

Ratified 2026-07-14 by the design authority. The map is fixed in DESIGN.md §Iconography — canonical, one-to-one, and not open for reinterpretation; this card renders it. Every status anywhere in the product uses the glyph below for its role — there is no second mapping, and a design that invents one is a defect.

The mapping

Role Outline — the default Filled Lucide name Why this glyph
success — circle-check A completed transition. The check is unambiguous and reads at 16px.
info — info Noteworthy, not urgent. Shares the circle silhouette with success — both are calm states.
warning triangle-alert The triangle breaks the circle silhouette — shape alone now separates "attend to this" from "noted".
destructive octagon-alert The stop-sign octagon. Blocking, and distinct in shape from the warning triangle.
stale — clock-alert Stale is about age, not failure. The clock says the data is old; the alert mark says that is a problem.

Rules

Outline is the default. Filled variants are reserved for warning and destructive — they are the colour-independent weight channel, which is what makes the two most urgent rungs legible without hue. A status icon is always paired with a text label. One family (Lucide), one stroke weight, sizes from the type scale. Icons carry meaning or are omitted — there are no decorative icons in this product.
